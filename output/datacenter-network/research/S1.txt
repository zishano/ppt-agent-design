2026 Ethernet Roadmap - Ethernet Alliance Search for: Blog Member Login Search 2026 Ethernet Roadmap For more than a decade, the Ethernet Roadmap has been the industry’s trusted guide to Ethernet’s ongoing evolution. As the world’s only publicly available Ethernet roadmap, it gives engineers, developers, network operators, and business leaders a clear view of both today’s technologies and what lies ahead. Charting Ethernet’s Next Era The 2026 Roadmap details Ethernet’s expanding role in AI, automotive, enterprise, cloud, manufacturing, and next-gen connectivity. Arriving at a key moment in Ethernet’s 50+ year history, it outlines the technologies and trends reshaping performance, power efficiency, and scale in an AI-driven world.
