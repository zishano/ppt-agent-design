Overview
This release includes significant new functionality and normative requirements.  It also includes a set of
editorial corrections and clarifications to improve the readability and accuracy of the Ultra EthernetTM
Specification v1.0.3, published on July 16, 2026.

Impact Statement
This revision defines new optional enhancement updates to protocol functionality and associated
requirements while preserving full backward compatibility with previous versions. It introduces a new
Unified Local profile with mandatory requirements. Clarifications and editorial updates to existing
functionality are non‑substantive and do not alter protocol behavior, compliance, or interoperability.
Correction updates are normative and required for conforming implementations.
